# Retail Atlas — second gap analysis against the current app

Reviewed November 12, 2026, after the Today hero and Prospecting/Growth reorganization.

**The main Retail destinations now exist. The biggest remaining gaps are customer-context continuity, the current app's prioritization and signal coverage, and the depth of the actual Retail records inside the new screens.** The next pass should complete those workflows within the existing tabs.

## Basis and limits

This is a fresh comparison of the current ReactRetail/shared source with the current Atlas files, supplemented by local browser inspection of ten Retail destinations and all nine Kyle relationship tabs. The browser inventory is `output/playwright/retail-gap-audit/inventory.json`. These 19 surfaces loaded without page errors; this was an inventory check, not acceptance testing of every action.

No new authenticated production-app browser session, org query, write or deployment was performed. A source implementation is not proof that the deployed endpoint or every populated state works today. The previous [deployed-app review](../2026-11-11-cockpits/LIVE_APP_REVIEW.md) remains dated evidence; its unsettled deep 360 views are not treated as verified live content.

Statuses below distinguish **covered in design**, **partial**, **missing**, and **integration-only**. Captured data and local draft actions do not establish production parity. No completion percentage is assigned.

## Verified continuity issues deserve attention before more visual expansion

1. **Same customer, different offer story:** main Growth has Kyle scores; his relationship Opportunities view reports no scored offer. The lookup paths are different, not proof that the source offer disappeared.
2. **Known holdings are not a financial spine:** the added cohort holding rows support owned-product cells but are not joined into the relationship financial-account ledger with balances. Their existence must not be confused with complete account coverage.
3. **Retail detail relies on Wealth examples:** the new trade ledger, transaction example, conversation trail and source notebook are populated for Julie. They do not establish a completed Retail customer workflow. Browser inspection confirmed the Kyle unavailable states.
4. **Lead source can be viewed but not preserved through the full create/edit form:** New lead or referral currently has neither source nor estimated value fields, despite current LeadModal support.
5. **First move has a different meaning:** the hero's case-first sample ordering is a useful presentation example, but it changes the current app's recent-browser/Who-to-Call selection contract. Next priority does not implement Snooze.

These are findings for the next design pass; this audit does not change the mock or production implementation.

## Recommended next three passes

### 1. Complete the daily Retail workflow

**Today → signal/queue → exact customer 360 → preparation/action → Workbench.** Start by carrying a real Retail customer's offers, holdings, conversations and next step across these destinations. Add the existing ranked queue, weekly net flow/attention context and recent agenda. Resolve contradictory empty states before expanding the dashboard further.

Acceptance: the same Account/product/source IDs and values survive every step; source scope remains explicit; the customer page never claims an already-shown score was not captured; a saved local follow-up returns to that customer and Workbench. In production migration, also verify real write/failure/refetch behavior.

### 2. Complete the business-development workflow

**Prospecting → warm introduction or CRM Lead → selected context → follow-up/Lead action**, and **Growth → opportunity evidence → existing/new Opportunity**. Add referrer context, Lead source/value, mover/outcome tracking and meaningful home-lending/investment cohorts. Use current write capabilities; do not imply actual Lead conversion or Opportunity stage editing is already supported.

Acceptance: introductions and Leads remain different record types; all current Lead fields survive editing; open/won cross-sell counters use the existing source definitions; whole-book filters are not limited to the 15 visible sample customers.

### 3. Complete the signal and risk coverage

**Intelligence → source-separated evidence → Today/Health/customer context**. Add liquidity, inflows, campaign and screening/sentiment changes; expand Health with property exposure and external service friction. Every signal needs a customer, date, reason and existing action, including true empty/unavailable states.

No additional primary tab is recommended. The present hierarchy can hold these capabilities: Today prioritizes, Prospecting acquires, Growth develops, Intelligence explains signals, Health watches risk, Relationships provides customer depth, Life & goals handles milestones, and Workbench executes.

## Preserve source truth; do not copy incumbent limitations

- The current Portfolio Pulse renders Wins · 30d as a fixed `$0`; Activity · 7d uses `data.schedule.length`, not a proven rolling seven-day completion calculation. Do not reproduce these as trustworthy new analytics without fixing/defining the source calculation.
- Current DetailsPanel edits are component-local; current TearsheetBuilder's Generate handler uses a local timeout. Actual persistent Account editing and prompt-generated output are enhancements, not established production parity.
- The current real customer fetcher does not establish a complete relationship network. The new book atlas is a navigation visualization, not proof of household/legal/referrer edges. Warm introductions have their own specific relationship-graph source contract.
- The existing bridge supports Opportunity creation; the mock's stage-scenario board is not evidence of actual stage-update support. Actual Lead conversion is also not established by the inspected write contract.
- Retain current score/expected-value thresholds, owned-product semantics, renter/property gates, source windows and CRM-vs-Data-360 trend semantics. Do not invent history, appointments, referrals, quotas or outcomes to fill a richer design.
