# Wealth household review studio

Adds the original tearsheet's useful section/tone/prompt controls to Atlas's exact-household folio and private questions. The preview is assembled deterministically from captured records. It does not claim an AI request, CRM save or sent packet.

Eight independently selectable sections cover relationship summary, financial accounts/outside investments, provider plan/CRM goals, eligible product conversations, risk/concentration, engagement, campaigns and source notes. Concise, Conversational and Detailed tones change the preparation copy; the proposed prompt is inspectable. Private questions and section/tone choices persist under org + captured advisor + exact Account keys. Existing legacy folio questions/sections are retained for the current dated study, and native `wa-folio-questions`, `data-wa=save-folio` and `wa-paper` workflows continue to work.

The studio appears on household Notes & tools and Notebook and in the native Build review folio / Prepare conversation entry. Notebook retains the original separate read-only Source notes panel. Other relationship tabs, source editors, Capital, Pipeline, Workbench and admin shell are delegated unchanged.

Download HTML creates an actual local standalone file with its source dates, Account and selected sections. Open print view creates a separate local paper with a Print control. Neither operation sends data to Salesforce. A full backend AI/document workflow remains a separate runtime contract; the original tearsheet's timer was not treated as genuine generation.

Data integrity: exact Account filters throughout; known-owned products excluded from acquisition suggestions; provider goal totals remain distinct from CRM goal funding; mortgage/account balances are not summed into AUM; market values/weights and screening observations retain their meanings. Missing sources remain unavailable; successful-empty source notes/campaigns remain empty. Fresh `wealthCustomerEvidence.sources.customer_*` envelopes are authoritative for details, financial accounts, plans, screening/property, satisfaction, predictions and engagement. Org, owner and allowed Account IDs are checked; fresh empty/unavailable never falls back to an older row. Standalone fallback remains available when the supplemental source itself is absent. Observed CSAT and individual NPS responses remain separate from predicted scores; rating descriptions are labels, not invented customer quotations. Digital engagement, complaints and segment are source input context, not causal attribution weights. Missing model periods are explicit; Gong profile week and actual last-call date remain distinct.

The binder and paper composition follows Atlas's existing purple/plum palette and system typography. The paper has a compact four-field ledger on wide screens; phone customization initially collapses so the packet is reachable before a long checklist. Customization remains open when changing fields in a phone session.

Integration: load `wealth-review-packet.css` and `wealth-review-packet.js` after the Wealth/customer facade; wrap last with `wealthAtlas=window.enhanceWealthReviewPacket(personaApi,wealthAtlas)`. No base-file edits or hooks are required.

Validation: `tests/wealth-review-packet.browser.cjs` checks eight household/tab layouts at1512/1024/768/390, section/tone/question persistence, household isolation, source fields, real HTML download contents, printable popup, native folio and Source notes, phone customization behavior, fresh CSAT/model context, and an authoritative empty-plan scenario despite an older plan being present. It records zero browser exceptions/network writes and saves screenshots under `output/playwright/wealth-review-packet/`.
