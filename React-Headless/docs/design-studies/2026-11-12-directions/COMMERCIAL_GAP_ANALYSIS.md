# Commercial Atlas — gap analysis against the current app

Reviewed November 13, 2026, against source at `539d4d329` and the local Atlas mock. This is a Commercial-specific design audit, not a deployed-app certification or permission to migrate production.

**The Commercial direction is recognizable and useful, but its operating depth still trails the current source.** Keep the company-first treasury lanes, deal room, payment-watch bands and Company Intel. Prioritize exact-company continuity, real blended credit priorities, the wider market/risk feeds, and owner-to-Wealth introductions before adding more generic cards.

## Commercial design proposal

Use a **company relationship command desk**, not a recolored Retail customer inbox. Today should place relationship defense and credit/payment context before treasury origination; operating cash and pending deals stay within the first useful screen. Growth keeps the Operate / Optimize cash / Fund growth composition, with real grouped product semantics and a business-development mode for company introductions and owner-Wealth handoffs. Intelligence organizes independent company market/credit/source events; Credit & coverage ties book cohorts to companies and exact source periods. The deal room owns the preparation and follow-through for a selected Opportunity, while company service and recorded activity remain distinct.

Keep dense, readable Atlas typography and useful visuals: a source-driver credit reading, payment cohort strips, paired cash-in/out observations, selected-company value/history plots and a company/source event timeline. Avoid horizontal customer ribbons as the only navigation and avoid empty full-width charts. Use search-first company lookup and four-to-eight-record working pages. No geography, forecasts, company relationships or credit covenants may be inferred from decorative graph placement.
