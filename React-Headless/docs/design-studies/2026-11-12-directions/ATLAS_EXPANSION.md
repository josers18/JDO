## Implemented views and interactions

- **Relationship:** Profile with exact record identity and local field-change proposal; trade ledger with side/search filters and execution ticket; conversation thread with topic filter and six-at-a-time paging; CSAT history and individual recommendation responses; selectable source meeting notes; dated WorldCheck screening; packet section/tone choices, assembled preview and local packet draft.
- **Retail:** owner-scoped retention trail with the largest-inflow institution selected using the production row-ranking rule, total outside-deposit flow/balance context, and persistent-stress samples. Six High markers summarize the captured six-month aggregate; they are not invented monthly observations.
- **Wealth:** six upcoming MoneyGuidePro reviews with source dates, modeled success, goal amount and plan-update date; Julie's deeper review journey; two real captured outside-platform balances/flows. Reviews are not booked appointments.
- **Commercial:** four D&B payment bands, all six ZoomInfo revenue bands, and six selected ESRI markets. The market scatterplot uses actual distance/wealth-index coordinates and count-scaled markers, with keyboard-selectable dots and a ZIP directory. Current D&B payment-band data has account keys and is distinct from legacy non-joinable loan-delinquency data.
- **Workbench:** task update/completion/deletion/reassignment proposals; final-action review; exact Account ID fields in forms and draft editing; meeting location, duration, Show As and planning timezone. A reviewed plan is invalidated when draft content changes. No captured Task/Case/Event changes or network writes are made by these actions.

Exact Account IDs, not names, associate new drafts with relationship tools. Existing name-only drafts remain visible in the full local draft library and need explicit linking before final-action review. Changes to the local relationship display name do not mutate the Account. No live-integration parity is claimed.
