# Commercial production acceptance gates

These are future production gates, not claims of deployed acceptance. Local browser results cover mock interactions only. Run after separately authorizing the production migration.

1. **Scope and actor:** verify current org, signed-in actor and selected banker; Allen's aggregates do not appear as another banker's book. An empty selected book remains empty/scoped. Cross-sell viewed-owner policy and general write ownership are tested independently.
2. **Exact identity:** two identically named companies and multiple deals under one company remain separate through search, provider evidence, selected product, preparation, proposal, Workbench, Back and reload. Retargeting an Account clears incompatible product/deal/source/referral provenance on the first save and later edits.
3. **Credit semantics:** source-model owner explicitly resolves predictor direction before publishing a directional label. PAYDEX/supplier payment days remain separate from bank delinquency. No severity/rank-derived health/drop is presented as observed history. Independent aggregates remain distinct from sampled constituents and missing profiles.
4. **Native work and cash:** driver weights/order, native Task/Opportunity derivation, exact-account dedupe, reserved market item, due buckets, snooze/refill, missing probability and signed business net flow match approved source behavior. Historical source Tasks/Events remain historical.
5. **Company Intel:** verify two companies with provider depth, a successful SEC no-match, a populated SEC filing with real identifiers/text when available, provider error/retry and mixed freshness. Missing sources never borrow Cameron's or Oliver's values. A successful-empty source does not assert no company risk.
6. **Source-specific intelligence:** independently test competitor/intent, champion source/person/new employer, funding (including future dates), outlook, leadership, ESG, Gong, AML Changed versus New/null-prior flags and marketing. No source is relabeled a completed banking or compliance decision.
7. **Treasury:** twelve grid rows include three actual Corporate Card holders. Owned overrides score; grouped products select native eligible rank/EV; held-away reasons match the same product; histories and book-wide movers retain their periods and thresholds. Zero open grid outcomes and nonzero Won results remain source data after a local draft.
8. **Origination:** real Lead source ID differs from directed referral target/referrer/business and owner/person Account IDs. Verify missing contact name/email, repeated target names, existing ownership, graph confidence, legacy source/status, blank versus zero AnnualRevenue and complete Lead edit/review. No Account ID is passed as Contact/Lead WhoId.
9. **Activities:** source Task/Event update/delete retains original object/record ID, original assignee and supported links. Event UTC round-trip works across time zones; duration/Who update restrictions are visible. Source changes do not insert new Events or create planned appointments. New meeting proposals have their own calendar and remain local until confirmed execution.
10. **Company depth:** finances use actual source balances/status and transactions; no aggregate balance is inferred from partial holdings. Source interactions, campaigns, notes, CRM Cases and external service preserve empty/unavailable states. Goal creation requires verified plan ownership, date-only fields do not shift a day, and owner personal context remains distinct from company financing.
11. **Service boundary:** external ServiceNow reference cannot reach the CRM Case update path. CRM Case and Opportunity source updates remain unavailable until explicitly supported by the bridge. No facility/covenant/borrowing-base/guarantor/team state is fabricated.
12. **Write lifecycle:** definite rejection shows error and retains the proposal with no success refetch; successful writes refetch exactly after success; timeout ambiguity does not blindly retry/duplicate. Validate permissions, IDs, recipients, duplicate submission and blank-field semantics against actual client/bridge responses.
13. **Shell/AI/config/theme:** multi-object search, waffle, notifications, Tasks, avatar/admin/setup/Data Cloud links work. ACC switches by teardown/re-embed; record grounding is verified independently of the label. Config/model catalog/theme definitions/per-user activation/display size survive real read/write/reload and permission failures.
14. **Navigation and privacy:** production URL/Back restores company/tab/product/deal/search/cohort/source/filter and Workbench context. Drafts/notes are isolated by org and actor with an approved persistence model; sessionStorage is not a security boundary. No stale data appears during book/persona changes.
15. **Usability/regressions:** 1512/1024/768/390px, keyboard tabs/dialogs/search/charts, readable essential labels and visible focus. Customer/source lists do not require a horizontal ribbon or dozens of pagination pages. No page-level overflow, runtime errors or unintended writes. Retail, Wealth, original supported routes, tools/prompt slots and production shell contracts pass regression.

Local evidence: `commercial-core.test.cjs`, `commercial-source.test.cjs`, `commercial-journeys.browser.cjs`, `commercial-edge.browser.cjs`; screenshot/report artifacts under `output/playwright/commercial-journeys`. Cross-persona QA is coordinated separately. None of these local gates certifies live CRM writes, permissions, ACC or deployment.
