## Art direction

| Direction | Color tokens | Typography | Composition and signature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Panorama | Cobalt #234BE8, white #FFFFFF, cloud #F9FBFF, slate #182534, blue mist #E9EDFF | System sans, 48–82px display, 20px introductory text, 17–18px body | A broad cobalt field. A large holdings visual shares the first screen with a readable relationship story; an open relationship shelf follows. Left-aligned copy and generous horizontal space. |
| Relationship atlas | Lilac #F0F1FB, lavender #E6E8F7, iris #5148AA, ink #282953, white #FFFFFF | System sans, 46–60px display, 18px body, 20px client names on desktop | A spatial canvas connects selected clients to the advisor's book, paired with a prominent evidence panel. Position is navigation, never a financial encoding or a claim about relationships between clients. |
| Advisory focus | Plum #362E42, paper #FCFAF6, mauve #886179, peach #F7D8BF, stone #EEE8E4 | System sans, 40–65px display, 18–20px body | An open relationship rail beside one spacious advisory document. A single client issue, its evidence, and conversation preparation lead the surface. |

The working choice is to compare the visual ideas using Wealth first. That is an exploration decision, not approval to impose one layout across Retail, Wealth, and Commercial. Body text stays at 17–20px; metadata is generally 15–16px. The 13px advisor label inside the mobile atlas center is an exception. Most controls are at least 44px tall. Type uses the existing OS-native stack and no external font download.

The plan was checked against the rejected study: remove the narrow multi-column dashboard, the 8–11px text, repeated small KPI boxes, and the assumption that palette swaps create distinct experiences. Each direction uses a different main interaction or spatial structure. No decorative price chart, invented history, fake portrait, or fabricated book total was added.

## Data and interaction

Shared inputs are the previous study's `study-data.js` and `crm-snapshot.js`. Values are historical, from the real-row fixtures and the read-only CRM capture on Nov 11–12, 2026. The same four selected Wealth relationships appear in all three directions. They are examples, not the full book.

- Switch directions without leaving the page; direct URLs use `?direction=panorama`, `atlas`, or `focus`.
- Select a relationship in the atlas or advisory rail to change the evidence context.
- Open relationship details and prepare editable conversation notes. Notes are authored study content; no AI endpoint is called. Saving keeps them in this tab's session storage only.
- Open Pipeline to inspect the saved owner-scoped aggregate and stage amounts. No synthetic trends are drawn.
- Open the capability directory for the integration preservation contract. Full 360, writes, themes, and Agentforce are not implemented by this exploration.

The existing [functionality preservation map](../2026-11-11-cockpits/FUNCTIONALITY_MAP.md) remains binding for any production implementation. A visual direction must be selected and expanded before it can satisfy that map.
